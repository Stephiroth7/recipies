## PIE CRUST (all butter)
https://www.seriouseats.com/old-fashioned-flaky-pie-dough-recipe

The ingredient amounts are supposed to be ratios.
The flour and butter are a 1:1 ratio by weight. 
The water amount is half of the flour amount by weight.

*INGREDIENTS*
* flour
* frozen butter in small cubes
* ice water
* salt (up to 1 tsp)
* sugar (optional, up to 1 TBSP for sweet pies)


* rolling pin - (blue, orange, or green bumper)
* plastic wrap or parchment paper to roll out dough between
* extra tablespoon of flour for rolling


*PREPARATION*
1. In a medium bowl, combine the dry ingredients until evenly distributed.
2. Add the butter to the flour mix. Try to make sure they're all separated and not clumped together.
3. Mix the flour over the butter cubes to coat them completely.
4. Use fingers or fist to smash the butter cubes into the flour mix.
5. Once the flour-butter mix is clumpy, begin to add water. Start with a small amount.
6. Lay down a sheet of parchment paper on a large flat surface. Flour the surface if you want.
7. As soon as the mixture holds together, take it out of the bowl and transfer to the parchment-covered flat surface.
8. Place a sheet of parchment paper on top and begin to roll out the dough. It's ok to have visible butter chunks.
9. Fold the dough in half from left-right then fold in half from top-bottom, and roll out again.
10. Aim for a thickness of between ⅛" and ¼", or between 3mm and 6mm.

At this point, you can either place the flattened dough directly into the pie plate (or baking tray) and chill it for at least 1 hour.
OR
Chill the dough rounds in the fridge for at least 1 hour, then let it come to barely room temp so it's foldable without breaking. Then line the pie plate (baking tray).

10. Chill the assembled pie (with filling & top) for at least 30 minutes before putting in the oven so that its butter doesn't leak out.

NOTES:
* try to make sure the pie crust is _always cold_.
* 1¾ cups (225 g) flour will yield enough for a 9-inch double crust pie. Make 14-inch rounds of dough.
* 1¼ cups (150 g) of flour will yield enough for a 6-inch double-crust pie. Make 9-inch rounds of dough.
