## CHOCOLATE GLAZE

**INGREDIENTS**
(makes 125 mL)
* 3 TBSP cocoa powder
* 2 ½ TBSP butter
* ½ cup confectioners' sugar 
* ½ vanilla
* 1 TBSP hot water

(makes 250 mL)
* 6 TBSP cocoa powder
* 5 TBSP butter
* 1 cup confectioners' sugar 
* 1 vanilla
* 2 TBSP hot water

**PREPARATION**
1. Melt cocoa and butter together in a small saucepan over low heat, stirring until smooth. Remove from heat once fully melted.
2. Whisk in confectioners' sugar and vanilla extract. 
3. Gradually whisk in hot water, 1 teaspoon at a time, until glaze is thick and smooth.
